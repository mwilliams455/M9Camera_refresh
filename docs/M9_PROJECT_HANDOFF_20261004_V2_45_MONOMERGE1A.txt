M9 CAMERA — 2.45 MONOMERGE1A HANDOFF
Date: 2026-10-04
Status: compiled, signed integration candidate; phone validation pending.

WHAT CHANGED
M9 and Monochrom are installed choices in the lower-right rendering picker.
M10-R and M11 remain unavailable. The existing M9 camera overlay stays in place.
Switching renderers recreates the camera view and clears renderer-specific live state.
Renderer switching waits for countdown/capture/bracket/final-save completion.

SETTINGS
There is one settings hierarchy with the selected renderer's relevant controls.
M9 Image: existing saturation, contrast, sharpness and presets.
Monochrom Image: contrast, sharpness, toning hue (Sepia/Cool/Selenium), strength (Off/Weak/Strong).
M9 and Monochrom remember independent image settings. Monochrom Auto ISO and bracket controls use their own keys.
Display uses luminance for Monochrom, including clipping warnings. Shared M9 RGB preferences are retained.
Monochrom AE-L and planned bracketing are Photo-mode features, as in its imported exposure planner.
Photo/Motion remain independent capture choices. No HDR or multi-frame image merging was added.

OUTPUT
JPEG, DNG+JPEG, and DNG work through the selected renderer.
Monochrom DNG retains the original linear SOURCE1D export; JPEG contrast/sharpness/toning are not baked into its pixels.
Monochrom Original Sensor RAW is an independent optional Bayer/color DNG, default off.
M9's accepted optional unfiltered-DNG behavior is retained.
Diagnostics are optional/default off. Enabled Monochrom runs include capture/renderer evidence and _MONO_PRIMARY.json.
The imported dark-frame policy uses supported hot-pixel metadata and guarded corrections on the derived path, with no second exposure, artificial delay or sensor-RAW edits.

CAPTURE OWNERSHIP
Requests bind immutable renderer/settings snapshots without replacing exposure-plan request tags.
The Monochrom save queue also freezes physical-camera parameters, owns one RAW per photograph, and closes it before waiting on durable DNG publication.
Final save tickets include JPEG metadata, selected DNG publication and optional sensor RAW.
Brackets advance only after their photograph's final-output ticket completes.
