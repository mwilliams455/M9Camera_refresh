# M9Cam 3.12 FOCUSOVERLAY1B — compact eye-local marker correction

**Parent:** Signed 3.11 FOCUSOVERLAY1A, SHA-256 `e6d63e7016193acbef9fd59fa8b3fc674b6bd7cb0bae522f294715b34372849d` from successful run 40389004859.

**Device evidence:** 27 October video `63341.mp4`. The four separate white corners and lack of black Eye AF plaque match the locked silhouette. However, in profile views and between ~6–18s the AF-region-derived overlay is visually too broad, often stretching forehead-to-nose. The marker center is aligned approximately to a detected eye and should not be shifted without new detector/coordinate evidence.

**Correction:** In `HumanSubjectFocusController` retain the exact focus target and original Camera2 update. Apply a display-only maximum of 17dp horizontal half-size and 13dp vertical half-size, via `FocusTickGeometry.eyeDisplayHalfWidth/Height`. Existing 10dp minimum in the shared geometry renderer handles tiny distant faces. Active white, pending/hold/lost amber, TalkBack strings and all non-eye tracking/spot/tap-focus indicators remain unchanged.

**Not addressed by this patch:** Intermittent Eye AF / No subject switching seen in the video (investigate independently after a focused AF test); the persistent yellow/magenta diagonal horizon-like guide and red clipping/zebra graphics, which remain even when no eye is detected and are not generated by this patch. No renderer, metering, ML pipeline or focus policies are changed.

**Test:** Pure-Java host tests cap/retention under different dp scales, geometry and unchanged target centre; inherited 3.11 focus and capture tests. Build/sign new 3.12 candidate from exact signed 3.11, verify signature, 16 KiB alignment, 37 libs and 301 assets, both menus. Keep 3.11 and main unchanged until user device validation of 3.12.
